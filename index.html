How it works

From idea to scheduled post in three steps.

  1. Describe your idea

    Type what you are promoting, or pick a template. Add your brand colors, logo and tone once and we remember them.

  2. Review what we make

    Get a caption, images, a voiceover and a short video. Change any part, or regenerate just one piece.

  3. Publish or schedule

    Send it to your social accounts now, or pick the best time and let ClipForge post for you.

Questions people ask

Do I need design or video skills?

No. You describe your idea and ClipForge creates the content. You can edit anything it makes.

Will the content match my brand?

Yes. Your logo, colors and tone are saved in your brand kit and used on every post.

Which platforms can I publish to?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, LinkedIn and X, with more planned.

Is AI-made content labeled?

Some platforms require an AI label on generated media. ClipForge will remind you when a label is needed.

How do I sign up?

Press Continue, choose Sign up, and register with Google or your email.
